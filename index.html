📷 Photo Finder — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Summary: Photo Finder processes your photos entirely on your device. Your photos never leave your phone. We collect only anonymous technical telemetry and advertising identifiers to measure app performance.

1. Introduction

Photo Finder ("the App") is a mobile application that allows you to search your photo gallery using natural language descriptions. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data.

2. Data Collection

Photo & Query Privacy: We do not collect, upload, store on external servers, transmit, or share your photos, search queries, or personal files. All photo indexing, search matching, and AI processing take place 100% locally on your device.

App Telemetry & Marketing Attribution: The App does not have user accounts or personal user profiles. We integrate third-party mobile measurement tools (Meta / Facebook SDK) that collect non-personally identifiable technical telemetry (such as app activations, feature interactions like search completions and checkout initiation) alongside device advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID) solely for application analytics, marketing attribution, and advertising campaign optimization.

3. Photo Access

The App requests permission to access your device's photo gallery solely for the purpose of:

  • Scanning and indexing photos using on-device neural networks
  • Displaying search results within the App

Your photos are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared. All image processing (neural network inference, embedding generation, and similarity matching) happens entirely on your device.

4. On-Device Processing

All core functionalities of the App, including photo analysis and search, are executed entirely on your device. The App relies on on-device machine learning models and local storage to analyze image content and process your search queries without transmitting your photos or text to external servers.

5. Network Usage

The App may use the internet connection for the following purposes:

  • Language model download: The App may download an offline translation model (~30 MB) when you first use the search feature in a non-English language. This is a one-time download.
  • Anonymous telemetry and ad attribution: Transmitting non-personal app events (e.g., app opens, checkout interactions) and device advertising identifiers to Meta servers for performance measurement.

No user photos, search queries, embeddings, or personal files are ever transmitted over the network.

6. Data Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Photo embeddings — Numerical vector representations of your photos (not the photos themselves), stored in a local database
  • Language models — Downloaded models for offline translation capabilities

All locally stored data can be removed by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data in your device settings.

7. Third-Party Services

The App may utilize third-party modules:

  • Offline Translation: Downloaded models process translations locally on the device and do not send search queries to external servers.
  • Meta SDK (Facebook SDK): Utilized for app event telemetry, install attribution, and advertising campaign analytics. To learn more about Meta's data practices, please visit the Meta Privacy Policy.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

9. Permissions

Permissions you are asked to grant. Android shows a system dialog for these, and the App works only with your approval:

  • Photos and media access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES and related media permissions) — To read your photo gallery for on-device indexing and search
  • ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION — To read photo metadata (not used for location tracking and never transmitted)
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — To show indexing progress while the App processes photos in the background

Permissions granted automatically by Android at installation. Android does not ask for these separately:

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — To download the offline translation model (only after you confirm it in the App) and to send anonymous analytics events to Meta
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_* — Used by the Meta SDK to read the device advertising identifier and Android advertising measurement APIs for ad attribution
  • BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE — Used by the Meta SDK to determine which advertising campaign led to the App installation
  • BILLING — To process the one-time Premium purchase via Google Play
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE, WAKE_LOCK — To keep photo indexing running while the screen is off

10. Your Choices and Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA)

Photos and search queries. Your photos, search queries, and photo embeddings are processed and stored only on your device. We have no access to them. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data in device settings permanently deletes all of this data.

Analytics and advertising data. The Meta SDK collects your device advertising identifier and technical app events as described in Section 2. Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), an advertising identifier may be considered personal data. We use this data only for app analytics and to measure the performance of our advertising campaigns. We do not sell this data and do not link it to your name, email, photos, or any other information that directly identifies you.

How to limit or stop this collection:

  • Delete or reset your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device). After deletion, apps can no longer use your advertising identifier.
  • Manage Meta data received from apps in your Meta account settings ("Your activity off Meta technologies").
  • Uninstall the App to stop all data collection immediately.

Your rights. Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, the UK, or California), you may have the right to access, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw any consent you have given. Since the App has no user accounts, we cannot identify you from the data stored by Meta. To exercise your rights, contact us using the email below, and you can also contact Meta directly via its Privacy Policy page.
